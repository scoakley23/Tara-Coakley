How it works

Handled, from the first idea to the last dance.

It starts with a conversation about the occasion, your guest list, and your budget. From there, I source and book the venue, florals, and vendors, all working off one shared timeline. Seating, signage, and the run-of-show get sorted well before the big day — so when it finally arrives, you get to be a guest at your own event instead of the one fielding vendor calls.
