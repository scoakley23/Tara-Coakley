How it works

A process that stays out of your way.

Every project starts with a conversation — walking the space together, talking through what isn't working, and getting honest about budget. From there, the design comes together and everything gets sourced before a single order goes in. Once renovation is underway, I coordinate the contractors and trades so the work happens in the right order and stays on schedule. Furniture and styling come last — the step that turns a renovated room into a home you actually want to live in.

Thinking about a renovation?

I'd love to see a few photos of the space and hear what you're hoping for — that's usually all it takes to get started.
