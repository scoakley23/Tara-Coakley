About

Hi, I'm Tara Coakley.

I've always had an eye for design — first in my own homes, then for other people's weddings and celebrations, and now through Oh What Fun. However it shows up, it comes down to the same thing: I love paying attention to the little details that make a space, or a day, feel exactly right.

The path here

My love of design started at home — quite literally. I've renovated my own houses more than once, including one true fixer-upper that slowly became our family's favorite place to be. There's nothing like living inside a renovation to teach you what actually matters: not just how a room looks, but how it holds up to real, everyday life.

Before I had kids, I worked as an event planner specializing in weddings and corporate events. After we moved to Wisconsin, I kept that love alive close to home — planning auctions, holiday parties, and fundraisers through our school community, a women's group, and a local foundation. Oh What Fun grew out of that same joy of pulling a great occasion together, just in pop-up form.
